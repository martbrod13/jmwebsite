What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved in your browser. Your browser’s local storage, which we also use, works in a similar way. They let the website remember that you are signed in, which time you chose while booking, your cookie settings and your language.

Which ones we use

TypePurposeConsent needed
Necessary: sign-in sessionKeeping you signed in to your accountNo
Necessary: booking in progressRemembering the chosen treatment and time, for example when you create an account during bookingNo
Necessary: cookie settingsRemembering your choice in the cookie bannerNo
Necessary: site languageRemembering whether you chose the Polish or English versionNo
Third party: Google MapsShowing the interactive map on the contact page. Google may set its own cookiesYes

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools.

How to change your settings

You can change your choice at any time in the . You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the necessary ones will stop signing in and online booking from working.
